1. Important Disclaimer (Private Consultancy Notice)

This portal solely assists contractors, employers, and HR teams in formatting their submitted payroll data into statutory templates (Form XV – Wage Slip and Form X – Employment Card). The legal accuracy, authenticity of employee wage data, authorized company seal/signature, and timely statutory distribution to workers remain the sole responsibility of the submitting employer/contractor.
2. Applicable Government & Labour Rules (Form X & Form XV)
  • Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Central Rules, 1971 (CLRA Rules):
    • Form X (Employment Card – Rule 76): Every contractor is legally required to issue an Employment Card in Form X to each worker within three days of employment.
    • Form XV (Wage Slip – Rule 78(2)(b)): Every contractor must issue a Wage Slip in Form XV to every workman at least one day prior to the disbursement of wages.
  • Code on Wages, 2019 & State Labour Laws: Employers and contractors must maintain accurate wage, attendance, and deduction records and provide physical or electronic wage slips to workers as prescribed under applicable Central/State labour rules (including Odisha Contract Labour Rules, where applicable).
  • Employer Statutory Obligation: Generating a formatted sheet through this portal does not complete statutory compliance unless the employer verifies all figures, affixes valid authorization, and distributes the forms to workers within statutory timelines.
3. IT Rules & Data Privacy Policy (IT Act, 2000 & DPDP Act, 2023)

This portal operates in alignment with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023:

  • Purpose Limitation: Employee data (names, wages, UAN/ESIC details, designations), company seals, and signatures uploaded via Excel/CSV or image files are collected strictly for generating Form XV and Form X documents and delivering them to your registered email ID.
  • Data Ownership & Employer Authorization: By uploading employee spreadsheets and company seals/signatures, the user warrants that they are an authorized representative of the company/contractor and have lawful authority to process the employees' personal and payroll data.
  • Data Retention & Confidentiality: Uploaded wage sheets, signature/seal files, and generated forms are stored securely on restricted Google Drive storage solely for order processing and verification, and are periodically purged. We never sell, rent, or share client or employee data with third parties.
  • Prohibition of Cyber Misuse (Sections 43 & 66 of IT Act, 2000): Uploading forged company seals/signatures, fabricated payment receipts, malware, or unauthorized third-party records is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate rejection and legal reporting.
